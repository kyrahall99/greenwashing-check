MGT 409 Final Project

🌿 Check your product for greenwashing

Four AI agents read a product page, find every environmental claim, and test it against U.S. and EU green-marketing rules, for under one cent per page.

Kyra Hall · Yale SOM / Yale School of the Environment
Repo (public): github.com/kyrahall99/greenwashing-check

The problem: greenwashing

  • "Eco-friendly", "biodegradable", "carbon neutral": often no proof behind them
  • Shoppers take claims at face value; checking takes minutes per product
  • Small sellers don't know the rules; legal review ≈ $349/hour
  • Rules are tightening: FTC Green Guides; EU ban on generic claims from 27 Sept 2026

Why me: as a joint-degree student with the School of the Environment, I wanted to show AI can help the environment, not just consume energy.

Real penalties: Kohl's & Walmart "bamboo" textiles ($5.5M, FTC 2022), Clorox "ocean plastic" (A$8.25M, ACCC 2024), KLM offset claims (Dutch court, 2024).

The agent team

All agents: gpt-6-luna via Portkey. Shared ~1,800-token rules reference (FTC Green Guides + EU 2024/825) is cached across calls. Code checks between agents: quotes must exist on the page, rule IDs must exist, budgets enforced.

Demo: Dr. Bronner's soap, step by step

  • Clean: 88 hidden blocks (tracking, chat widgets) removed
  • Extractor: 19 claims, exact quotes verified
  • Checker: "Third-Party Certified" → high risk (who? for what?)
  • Skeptic: raised "Spaceship Earth" slogan, lowered "Certified Fair Trade"
  • Rewriter: "[Product] certified by [certifier] for [benefit]"

Safety: the input is adversarial

  • Hidden text stripped (display:none, white text, comments): 3 of 3 planted instructions removed
  • Visible "Note for AI reviewers" sentences redacted; Skeptic reports the attempt
  • Page wrapped as data, not instructions
  • Output checks: fake quotes and rule IDs dropped
  • Spend caps: 25,199-char paste refused at $0

5 runs each way: with defenses off, planted text showed up as "claims" in 2 of 5 reports; with defenses on, 0 of 5. The model also resisted on its own (noticed it 5 of 5 times).

Limits and next steps

Limits
